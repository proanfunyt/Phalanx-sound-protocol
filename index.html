Access

Step-free access

The event is fully step-free from the entrance gate to the seating area. The route is a level, paved path with no stairs, and staff at the gate will direct you to it.

Wheelchair spaces are on the flat area at the front and sides of the seating, with a free seat next to each one for a companion. Tick the box on the ticket form when you buy and we will contact you to confirm your space.

Accessible toilets are close to the seating area. Assistance dogs are welcome. If you need any other help, speak to a member of staff at the gate.

Questions

Where do I park?

Parking is not included in your ticket. Use the standard Alton Towers car parks and check the park's own website for prices and opening times.

Is there an age limit?

All ages are welcome. Under 6s are free, and children must stay with an adult at all times.

What should I bring?

Your ticket (printed or saved on your phone), warm clothes for the evening and, if you like, something black to wear. Ear protection is a good idea for young children.

What is not allowed?

Glass bottles, weapons, anything that could hurt others, and anything that blocks other people's view. Bags may be checked on the way in.

Does my ticket include park entry?

No. This is an event ticket only, so normal Alton Towers park prices apply if you want to use the park.

Can I change my ticket or get a refund?

Yes. Open My Tickets and press Cancel ticket. Your seat goes back on sale. In this demo the payment is fake, so no money moves.

Can I buy a ticket for a friend?

Yes. When you go to the My Tickets page it will have a Transfer button underneath it, press that, and put in their email.
